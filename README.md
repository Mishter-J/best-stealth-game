Velvet Shadow — a top-down stealth game. You are Wren, a thief in a museum after midnight: sneak past patrolling wardens and their vision cones, hide in bushes, toss pebbles to lure guards away, pocket the three Starling relics, and slip out the green door unseen. Three halls, a detection meter that gives you a chance to recover, and footsteps that tell you where a warden is. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make the best stealth game” bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD/arrows to move, SHIFT to sneak, SPACE to sprint (loud), click to toss a pebble, ESC to pause, R to restart.
